### Marilo Meta

I design and build Salesforce systems: data models, automation, integrations, and the training that helps people use them. I work mostly with nonprofits, associations and growing organizations.

Currently Salesforce Business Systems Specialist at AMT, The Association For Manufacturing Technology. The repositories here are personal and independent work.

Re-authored case study with deployable source and a demo setup script.
- **[community-conference-kit](https://github.com/marilometa/community-conference-kit)**: a Salesforce data model and playbook for running a community-led conference, from call for papers to sponsors and volunteers.
